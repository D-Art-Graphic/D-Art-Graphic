Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our design process, deliverables, and terms.

Our process has four distinct phases: Discovery (researching your industry and target customer), Conceptualization (drafting initial sketches and options), Design Iteration (redefining selections based on your direct feedback), and Final Delivery (supplying all source files, guides, and exports).

We work with both fixed project-based pricing (for standard requirements like logo design, packaging, and brand identities) and monthly retainer arrangements (for ongoing social media branding, advertising banners, and video editing). We provide detailed quotes prior to kickoff.

Delivery depends entirely on project complexity. Individual assets (like posters, social media banners, and business cards) take 2-4 business days. Comprehensive brand identities and complex packaging projects typically take 2-3 weeks, with scheduled milestone previews.

We prioritize client satisfaction. Standard projects include 3-5 rounds of comprehensive feedback loops/revisions to refine shapes, colors, and typographies. We work closely with you at each step to ensure the results align perfectly with your vision.

We deliver complete high-resolution, print-ready, and digital-ready assets. This includes vector source files (.AI, .EPS, .PDF, .SVG) as well as standardized image formats (.PNG, .JPG) with transparent layers and style guide booklets.

Yes, we provide 30 days of complimentary support after final files are handed over. This includes addressing print sizing queries, providing additional file conversions, or assisting with deployment across platforms.
